ADITYA PORTFOLIO — VIDEO-INSPIRED VERSION

1. Open index.html in your browser.
2. Edit the text in index.html to change your name, bio, projects and email.
3. Replace the profile placeholder in the hero with your own image if desired.
4. Put your image at assets/profile.jpg and replace the .portrait-placeholder div in index.html with:
   <img class="profile-image" src="assets/profile.jpg" alt="Aditya Gola" />
   Then add .profile-image{width:100%;height:100%;object-fit:cover;border-radius:23px} to style.css.
5. The site is responsive and works without a build tool.

The design is an original recreation inspired by the uploaded reference video: dark background, oversized typography, rounded cards, animated role text, technology chips, project cards and a large contact section.
